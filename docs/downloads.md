### 📱 Installing the APK on Android
1. Click **Download APK** on the latest release above.
2. When the download finishes, tap the notification or open your device's **Downloads** folder.
3. If prompted, enable **"Install unknown apps"** for your browser in Android settings.
4. Tap **Install** and launch **NjiaSalama**.
